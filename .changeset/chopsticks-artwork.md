The chopsticks motif artwork is supplied as `Chopsticks-CN.svg` in the brand assets (T7.13, REQ-029, ADR-0020). The owner chose it from four candidates: a pair of pink chopsticks with yellow bands and a yellow sticker edge, drawn in `#ef80ae`, `#fff488`, and black outlines, with white only for the shine marks, on a transparent background. No component renders it yet, so nothing changes for a consuming app until a component uses the file.
