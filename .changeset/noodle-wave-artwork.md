The noodle curves and waves motif artwork is supplied as `Noodle-Wave-CN.svg` in the brand assets (T7.14, REQ-029, ADR-0022). The owner chose it from four candidates: four stacked waves with curled ends, drawn in `#ef80ae`, `#fff488`, and black outlines, with white only for the shine marks, on a transparent background. No component renders it yet, so nothing changes for a consuming app until a component uses the file.
