The golden d20 motif artwork is supplied as `D20-CN.svg` in the brand assets (T7.12, REQ-029, ADR-0021). The owner chose it from four candidates: a straight-on die with even black outlines, drawn flat in `#fff488` with no shine marks, on a transparent background. No component renders it yet, so nothing changes for a consuming app until a component uses the file.
