The pink ramen cup motif artwork is supplied as `Ramen-Cup-CN.svg` in the brand assets (T7.11, REQ-029, ADR-0019). The owner chose it from four candidates: a pink cup with a peeled lid, tall noodles, and a game controller beside it. It is drawn in `#ef80ae`, `#fff488`, and black outlines, on a transparent background. No component renders it yet, so nothing changes for a consuming app until a component uses the file.
