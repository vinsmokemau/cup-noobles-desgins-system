The sticker border motif artwork is supplied as `Sticker-Border-CN.svg` in the brand assets (T7.16, REQ-029, ADR-0024). The owner chose it from four candidates: a frame with a yellow outer edge, a pink inner band, and notched corners, drawn in `#ef80ae`, `#fff488`, and black outlines, with white only for the shine marks, on a transparent background and with an empty centre. `CnStickerFrame` does not render it yet, so nothing changes for a consuming app until the component uses the file.
