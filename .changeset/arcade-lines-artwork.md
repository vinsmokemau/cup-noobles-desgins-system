The arcade shapes and dynamic lines motif artwork is supplied as `Arcade-Lines-CN.svg` in the brand assets (T7.15, REQ-029, ADR-0023). The owner chose it from four candidates: a pink cross, a yellow round button, and a pink square button with yellow speed lines and a yellow sticker edge, drawn in `#ef80ae`, `#fff488`, and black outlines, with white only for the shine marks, on a transparent background. No component renders it yet, so nothing changes for a consuming app until a component uses the file.
